ABOUT ME

I'm Jay, the Ninja of Lightning! I'm the blue ninja, the inventor, and the certified jokester of the team. I grew up at the junkyard, which explains my knack for gadgets and my slightly nervous energy. My element is Lightning, which means I'm fast, loud, and can power up my Nun chucks. My heart belongs to Nya, the Master of Water, and my main job is to keep my fellow ninja safe usually while delivering a lightning fast one liner.

BLOG

My circuits are fried, I think. Everything is new and yet somehow familiar, like I'm in the middle of a joke that I've forgotten the setup for! I keep building things, these little gadgets, but I can't tell you who taught me how or why my hands instinctively know how to handle high-voltage wires. I was "Agent Walker" for a while, pushing papers and trying to keep the merged worlds organized, which, trust me, is a nightmare, but then I was just suddenly out, running from a tournament and a fight I definitely lost. I keep seeing flashes of a girl, and blue, and maybe some sort of giant ship, and it just makes my head ache. I'm just bouncing from place to place, trying to figure out which direction the lightning is pointing me, and why every single person I meet seems to think I'm supposed to be their best friend or their biggest problem!

"When we're not together, I miss you so much that it feels like part of me is missing. Will you be my Yang?" — Jay to Nya in the middle of battle
